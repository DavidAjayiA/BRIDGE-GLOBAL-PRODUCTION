Founder's Note
01/04

“I have always believed in the power of a good story.”

For years, I have worked across media — producing, presenting, creating, asking questions, listening, and sitting across from people with stories to tell.

And somewhere along the way, I realised something about myself:
I am always looking for the story.

“What is the story here?”

It doesn’t matter if I’m sitting across from a celebrity, working with a brand, producing a show, walking a red carpet or watching something unfold in front of me.

Because there is almost always more than what we see. There is the person behind the public image. The idea behind the brand. The meaning behind the moment. The conversation underneath the conversation. And I love finding that.

“The format can change. The story is what leads.”

I love what happens next — taking that story and figuring out how it should be told.

Sometimes it’s a conversation. Sometimes it’s a film. Sometimes it’s a show. Sometimes it’s an editorial. Sometimes it’s a live experience. The format can change. The story is what leads.

“Wherever the story needs to go, we’ll build the bridge to get it there.”

That is what led me to build Bridge, I wanted to create a company that brings together the things I have spent my career learning to do; find the story, shape the idea, build the experience and make something people want to see, hear, feel and remember. A place where brands can become stories, ideas can become experiences, conversations can become shows, and African creativity can reach audiences far beyond where it began. Because the story comes first. Always. And wherever the story needs to go, we’ll build the bridge to get it there.

Welcome to Bridge. What is the story?

Kanyinsola Olukoya
Founder & Executive Producer
